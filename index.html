02 / ABOUT ME

I like being close
to the whole problem.

AS↗

The pipeline, the business question, and the person who needs the answer.

At Finix, being part of a small data team means working across ingestion, modeling, reporting, and production support. I enjoy that breadth: understanding the problem, building the system, and staying accountable for how it works.

Before Finix, I worked on credit-risk data at JPMorgan Chase and researched market data at CME Group. That experience shaped how I approach engineering: make the numbers explainable, the systems dependable, and the handoff clear.

I also write technical plans and runbooks, build reusable validation tools, and help teammates get up to speed. Good infrastructure includes the knowledge people need to use it.
